# Notes on the packaged numbers

All CSV values are transcribed from the unpublished manuscript:

Kopp, M. B. *Printable Organic Light-Emitting Diodes for Transparent Display and Visualized Sensing.* Unpublished manuscript (2026), Maximilian B. Kopp.

- The manuscript contains no numerical tables. Process parameters and results appear only in the prose.
- There is no calibration-point table, luminance–voltage table, or humidity–intensity table, so slopes and the “up to 52%” sensitivity cannot be refit from the packaged files.
- Photographs and the architecture schematic were not digitized. No numbers were read off figures except the on-figure caption of Figure 3, which is qualitative.
- `oled-stated-results.csv` and `oled-fabrication-parameters.csv` are the author's stated values for this work.
- `oled-section6-market-projections-as-stated.csv` transcribes market figures from section 6; those are not device measurements.
- `literature-cited-values-not-this-work.csv` transcribes numbers that the literature review attributes to other papers.
